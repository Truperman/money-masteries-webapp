MONEY MASTERIES COMMERCE

Privacy, account connections & support

Updated November 25, 2026 · This page describes the Shopify product-export connector.

Money Masteries Commerce connects an existing eligible Money Masteries account to a Shopify store. General member installations remain unavailable while Shopify distribution and security review requirements are completed.

What the connector does

You choose products from your Money Masteries catalog and explicitly request an export to your connected Shopify store. Products are created as drafts for your review. Previously exported products are preserved on repeat exports. Review taxes, inventory, shipping, fulfillment and publication in Shopify.

Information used

  • Your Money Masteries member identifier and the Shopify store domain you authorize.
  • Encrypted Shopify access and refresh credentials, their granted permissions and expiration information.
  • The product information and creative media you select for export, including names, descriptions, prices, SKUs, images and supported videos.
  • Product identifiers and operation records needed to avoid duplicate exports and reconcile connection activity.

The connector requests product access. It does not request Shopify customer records, orders, payment details, payouts or banking data. Your Money Masteries account and membership information are handled separately from Shopify store customers.

How information is used and shared

Selected product information and media are sent to the Shopify store you authorize. Media you choose for export may be copied into Shopify. Connection records and encrypted credentials are stored on the Money Masteries backend using Google Cloud/Firebase services. Provider credentials are used on the server and are not returned to member pages.

Nothing is published to your storefront automatically by this export. Exporting products does not automatically create an advertising campaign, enroll you in a paid Shopify plan or establish an affiliate commission.

Connection choices and retention

You can disconnect Shopify inside Money Masteries or uninstall the app in Shopify. Disconnecting removes the active Money Masteries connection credentials. Uninstall and Shopify store-erasure webhooks remove matching connection and pending authorization records. Expired, unclaimed connection handoffs are removed by scheduled maintenance. Product-export identifiers are removed when Shopify sends a store-erasure request.

Uninstalling the Shopify connector does not automatically delete products or creative content you independently created in Money Masteries, cancel a separate Money Masteries membership, or remove a copy already imported into Shopify. You control imported products in Shopify and can request help with Money Masteries data below.

Membership, usage and referrals

The current connector workflow is for existing Elite Money Masteries members. Membership and activated provider usage are separate. The public-app billing arrangement must be accepted by Shopify before general distribution is enabled. We do not change or add a Shopify app charge during the connection test.

Shopify affiliate enrollment is separate from app installation. A new-store signup earns referral credit only under an approved affiliate program and its qualification rules. Money Masteries will identify an affiliate relationship when an approved tracking link is offered.

Support and privacy requests

Contact reachdavid.work@gmail.com for connection help, access or deletion requests. Include your store domain and the email address on your Money Masteries account so the request can be verified. Do not email passwords, API secrets, payment-card information or access tokens.
