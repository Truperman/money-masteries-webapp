# Creative Studio and Elite commerce

Money Masteries: Pro $50/month, one niche and 30% direct membership commissions. Elite $97/month, three niches, Business Automation, websites/ecommerce and Shopify product exports, 50% direct membership commissions. All education is free. Provider usage is separate from membership and from ProsHub.

## Delivered workflows
- Tools → Creative Studio: member-owned uploads, image/video generation quotes and durable jobs, Content Library, captioned campaign drafts, Zernio social connections, queue/scheduling, paused ad creation and separate budget activation.
- Browser video editor: FableCut trim operations, 30-second export, vertical/square/landscape, captions, floating headline, mute, poster image and MP4/WebM encoding. Local editing is included. Imported files can be 100 MB; library uploads/edited exports are limited to 6 MB.
- Elite Website & Store: business/ecommerce site drafts, own catalog up to 500 products, reusable creative images/videos, preview, versioned publication and unpublication. Each product uses the member's external secure checkout link; taxes/shipping/payments/fulfillment remain with that seller/provider. This release does not implement a native cart or fulfillment backend.
- Shopify CSV exports the entire owned catalog as drafts with prices, SKUs and images. Video remains in the library/site. Direct OAuth exports up to 200 products per request as drafts; repeats preserve previously exported Shopify products. It does not continuously synchronize edits, inventory or orders.
- Owner-only Shopify app setup and approved affiliate-link setup. OAuth ties browser state and HMAC to the initiating signed-in member. Offline tokens and app secrets are encrypted server-side. Verified uninstall/privacy webhooks remove Shopify credentials/export metadata; no Shopify customer/order data is read.

## Usage accounting
Existing dedicated Money Masteries Stripe usage subscriptions/meters are reused. The server derives member identity and entitlement from verified authentication, never a submitted member ID, browser tier or ProsHub business ID. Each provider operation reserves exposure transactionally before submission and records immutable, globally deduplicated provider receipts. Retries do not submit or charge a second time. Uncertain submissions retain a review hold rather than silently retry.

Generation requires both commercial and final price approval. The displayed authenticated provider quote × 5 is the member charge (400% markup). A changed quote stops submission. Provider-contract verification must confirm these fixed request prices cover the final provider charge before generation is enabled.

Zernio API connection/post/ad requests are included at zero per-request cost under its published pricing; actual account and managed-ad recurring fees are billed from each member profile's verified closed-cycle usage × 5. Neither the entire platform bill nor another profile's cost can be allocated to that member. Connections authorize up to $60 exposure per cycle, not an upfront $60 debit; managed-ad authorizations are $0.10 each. Member limits must cover these authorizations. The provider-confirmed receipt is frozen before billing so crash retries cannot select changed amounts. A one-minute sweep settles closed periods and stops platform-created ads/connections when paid access, authorization or provider eligibility fails. Manual stop/disconnect remains available after membership expiration. Outstanding holds/unknown provider outcomes require review; actual late invoices or costs outside the verified schema are never guessed.

Ad network spending is charged directly by the member's ad account. It is separate from Money Masteries service fees. Create paused, verify owner/currency/live budget/live end date, then require a separate activation confirmation. Final network review/delivery is not implied by a scheduled or accepted response. Stop operations affect ads created here; campaigns created outside this tool stay in the member's Ads Manager.

## Validation
Unit tests exercise billing/identity/ownership, price changes, rejection/uncertainty, OAuth/HMAC/browser/member binding, recurring cost scoping and deduplication. A real Firestore emulator tests concurrent provider submission, transaction conflicts, separate members' exposure and recurring settlement. Browser tests use a fixture backend for actual upload controls, saved form values, library/campaign/catalog/CSV flows, plan differences, account switching and 390px layout. Editor testing imports a synthetic clip, uses actual FableCut operations and encodes a playable H.264 MP4. Live provider generation/posting/advertising and Shopify merchant acceptance are not claimed by these tests.
