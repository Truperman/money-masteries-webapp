## Understand the funds

### 1. Follow the surplus lifecycle

Learning goal: Explain how a possible surplus becomes a verified claim.

#### The core idea

A property sale can generate proceeds beyond the obligations and costs paid through that sale. Depending on the sale and jurisdiction, the remaining money may be described as a surplus, excess proceeds, or an overbid. The label does not tell you who is entitled to receive it. Start by separating three events: a sale occurred, funds were deposited, and an authorized decision established who may receive them.

#### Put it into practice

Draw a chain from the sale official to the custodian, then to the claim process and finally to a documented disbursement. A county office, clerk, trustee, court, or state unclaimed-property agency may be involved. Those roles vary. An old advertisement or sale result supplies a research clue, not confirmation of a present balance. Keep the source date beside every fact.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

Our educational goal is accurate case administration. There is no standard payout, universal recovery fee, or guaranteed income. Dr. Gainz is a fictional AI educational presenter. His lab coat is a character design, not a professional credential.

Practice: Using a fictional case, draw the four-event chain: sale, deposit, entitlement decision, disbursement. Mark every missing event as unknown.

Knowledge check: Which statement establishes a currently payable claim?
1. A high auction price alone
2. A named owner on an old list
3. Current official balance and entitlement verification through the applicable process
4. A recovery business advertisement

Answer: 3. Sale proceeds and ownership clues alone do not establish present availability or entitlement.

### 2. Keep tax sales and mortgage foreclosures separate

Learning goal: Recognize different processes before choosing records or forms.

#### The core idea

A tax-sale process begins with unpaid public obligations. A mortgage foreclosure generally enforces a secured loan. Their offices, dates, redemption rights, priority rules, and claim forms can differ. Even within one state, judicial and nonjudicial processes may take different routes. Do not reuse a familiar form simply because both situations involve a house and money.

#### Put it into practice

Create a case-cover label with the state, county, sale type, responsible office, and source for that classification. Write down whether the sale is final and whether any relevant waiting or review period remains. If the supplied documents do not establish the process, pause classification and ask the responsible office or a qualified local professional to clarify.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

A responsible researcher distinguishes what a document says from what they infer. “Foreclosure” on a search result is too broad to select a claims procedure. Your next action should address the missing classification, not assume it.

Practice: Compare two fictional cover sheets: one tax sale and one mortgage foreclosure. List three questions that must be resolved before selecting a claim form.

1. Yes, if the owner signs
2. No, the sale type and local procedure must be established
3. Yes, for amounts under $50,000
4. Yes, if AI drafts it

Answer: 2. Different processes and jurisdictions can require different evidence, forms, and representation.

### 3. Define a service that creates real value

Learning goal: Describe a transparent service without promising a result.

#### The core idea

Useful support can include organizing records a client supplies, tracking missing information, explaining the source of a status update, and coordinating a professional review. That value depends on accurate work and permitted activities. Locating a possible fund does not automatically authorize you to represent a claimant, charge a fee, purchase rights, or file papers.

#### Put it into practice

Write a one-page service description: what you do, what you do not decide, what you charge only if permitted and agreed, and when you refer the matter. Explain that an owner may have a direct claim route. Show any prospective client where the official process is described, without suggesting that your business is the government or that payment to you is mandatory.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

Business income is uncertain. Training, tools, legal review, and time are costs. Avoid advertising a typical recovery or monthly income unless you have a sound basis for the specific claim. This course teaches a process, not an income promise.

Practice: Draft a plain-language service description with a direct-claim disclosure and a professional-referral boundary. Use no income claims.

## Choose a workable jurisdiction

### 4. Map official programs before researching cases

Learning goal: Build a county-level source inventory.

#### The core idea

Start with official program and procedure pages. Identify the agency name, sale type, official URL, update date, contact channel for the office, and any access limitations. County-level research can help you understand a market without compiling private individuals into financial prospect profiles. Keep third-party marketing claims separate from agency evidence.

#### Put it into practice

The companion research directory contains 75 program-source entries across 72 counties and 16 states, checked November 24, 2026. It is a starting directory, not a list of available claims or cash. Read each row’s limitations before relying on it. Separate historic, closed, login-limited, and current-procedure sources. Recheck dates rather than treating a research snapshot as a continuing feed.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

Do not infer “no funds” from an absent web list, or “funds available” from a link that exists. Record unknown availability honestly. Public access and permitted commercial use are separate questions.

### 5. Check record-use and fee restrictions first

Learning goal: Identify restrictions before designing outreach or pricing.

#### The core idea

Access to government information does not automatically permit every use of it. King County’s surplus page expressly warns against commercial use of lists of individuals. South Carolina law restricts knowingly obtaining or using personal government-record information for commercial solicitation. These are examples, not a complete national rule map.

#### Put it into practice

Keep a jurisdiction requirements sheet with the exact official source, the date reviewed, the activity being considered, and the unresolved question. Check access attestations, solicitation, representation, licensing, compensation, assignments, and retention. Do not click an acknowledgement that conflicts with your purpose or use an agent to cross a gate you could not truthfully accept yourself.

### 6. Build a jurisdiction checklist

#### Put it into practice

For each requirement, retain a short note and an official source link. Label the requirement as confirmed for the specific sale type, awaiting clarification, or outside your role. Do not copy an old deadline into a new case without verifying what event starts the clock. Source publication dates, sale dates, and claim deadlines are different fields.

Keep the checklist current without rewriting the underlying evidence. When guidance changes, preserve the former version date and explain what needs professional review. Your checklist organizes research; it does not decide a legal question.

## Begin with owner permission

### 7. Start with permissioned intake

Learning goal: Define what a prospective client authorizes.

#### The core idea

Use an owner-initiated or otherwise permissioned consultation instead of building a mass list of private people and financial balances. Explain your business identity and the purpose of the discussion. Ask what help the person wants before receiving documents. Consent to a conversation is not consent to market unrelated services, sign filings, sell information, or access every record connected to that person.

#### Put it into practice

Collect the minimum information needed for the next permitted task. Record the scope of authorization, the date, permitted sharing, and how it may be withdrawn. Avoid collecting identity numbers, bank details, or original documents before a justified need and a suitable secure process exist. Give the person a way to use the official claim process directly.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

A signed intake authorization is an administrative record. It is not an assignment of fund rights, a power to practice law, or an entitlement decision. Keep those concepts distinct and have any operative legal document reviewed locally.

Practice: Write a short intake-purpose statement and a permission checklist. Practice with fictional information only.

### 8. Verify who has authority to act

Learning goal: Recognize when identity and entitlement require professional review.

#### The core idea

The person who contacts you may be the former owner, a co-owner, a representative of a business, a relative, or someone assisting an estate. Those descriptions do not by themselves prove entitlement or authority. Keep an identity check separate from ownership history and from the right to receive a particular distribution.

#### Put it into practice

Ask for the specific documents relevant to the next review step through a secure channel. For an estate, entity, trust, or disputed ownership, record the issue and seek qualified guidance rather than guessing who can sign. Do not locate relatives, infer heirs, or decide probate rights through an AI-generated family tree. Work from authorized material and appropriate official findings.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

Maintain an authority field marked verified for the stated task, awaiting review, or not established. Preserve any limitation on sharing. Stop the workflow when someone seeks action beyond the authority documented in the file.

Practice: Classify three fictional intake examples: an owner asking about their own records, an adult child without authority documents, and an entity representative. List what remains unresolved.

### 9. Explain the direct-claim option

People should understand that a government or court process may be available directly. Explain what your business would add, if the activity and compensation are permitted. ## Build a reliable evidence file

### 10. Locate the current custodian

Learning goal: Trace transfers instead of assuming funds remain with a county.

#### The core idea

A source may describe a sale, an initial deposit, or an older holding account. None automatically identifies where money is held today. A file can involve a clerk, trustee, treasurer, court registry, or state agency. Identify the actual custodian and distinguish its accounting role from the role of a court deciding entitlement.

#### Put it into practice

For an authorized case, request or review the relevant official status through the applicable process. Record the response date, source document, balance definition, and whether any transfer or distribution occurred. If funds moved, keep both the old and new references. Do not turn a historical balance into a current one simply because a webpage still displays it.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

Use three separate fields: original reported deposit, current confirmed balance, and verification date. Leave missing values unknown. A written current response can still contain conditions or pending matters that must be preserved in your summary.

Practice: Trace a fictional county-to-state transfer. Identify which old figure cannot be labeled a current county balance.

### 11. Read the sale and distribution documents

Learning goal: Separate the bid, debt, costs, deposit, and disbursements.

#### The core idea

A winning bid, an opening bid, a judgment amount, and a deposit balance describe different things. Start a document index rather than subtracting whichever two numbers are easiest to find. Record what each figure represents and its effective date. Interest, permitted costs, refunds, or later distributions may affect the accounting.

#### Put it into practice

Use a fictional evidence table with columns for document title, source, date, figure label, amount, and limitation. Match a sale result to the appropriate official accounting. A ledger line should be traceable to a source; an AI summary is not the source itself. When a figure is missing or inconsistent, record a question instead of filling it with zero.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

North Carolina’s statute illustrates why costs and different obligations must be accounted for in a defined order. Other procedures require their own review. The educational arithmetic in this course must not replace the accounting or entitlement process.

Practice: Create a six-document index for a fictional file. Mark the opening bid as a bid figure, not a verified final debt.

### 12. Track claims, distributions, and deadlines

Learning goal: Preserve status changes and uncertainty.

#### The core idea

A filed motion or claim is not necessarily an approved distribution. Similarly, an approved distribution may have been paid, stayed, corrected, or challenged. Keep an event log that distinguishes filed, pending, ordered, disbursed, and unresolved statuses. Use the language of the source and preserve any qualification.

#### Put it into practice

For each important date, record the triggering event, the source, and who confirmed its significance. Flag a deadline that requires professional interpretation. Keep status history instead of overwriting a former amount without explanation. Where a source is old, closed, or access-limited, make that limitation visible at the top of the file.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

No general time promise is appropriate for every claim. A useful update states what was checked, when it was checked, what remains unknown, and the permitted next action. Avoid telling a client that money is on its way because a document was submitted.

Practice: Write a fictional event log containing a claim, a competing claim, and an unresolved hearing. Draft an accurate two-sentence status update.

## Analyze a case without guessing

### 13. Use illustrative surplus arithmetic

Learning goal: Calculate a learning residual while preserving missing inputs.

#### The core idea

For a classroom example, assume sale proceeds of $225,000, documented sale costs of $18,000, and a documented secured obligation of $172,000. The arithmetic residual is $35,000. This is an illustration of subtraction, not a determination that an owner is presently owed that amount. Real cases require the applicable accounting, priorities, and current balance.

#### Put it into practice

If a qualified review later establishes a further $13,000 distribution in the same fictional example, the illustrative residual after that allocation is $22,000. Do not subtract a disputed claim as though it is an approved payment, or ignore it as though it has no possible effect. Show alternate scenarios and label them clearly.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

The practice calculator accepts fictional inputs and refuses to treat blank entries as zero. Its output is an illustrative residual only. It does not verify funds, apply state law, decide lien priority, or calculate your fee.

Practice: Use the practice calculator with 225000, 18000, 172000, and 13000. Explain in writing why the $22,000 output is not a verified payout.

Knowledge check: What is the arithmetic residual after all four fictional entries?
1. $35,000
2. $22,000
3. $40,000
4. A guaranteed payment of $22,000

Answer: 2. 225,000 minus 18,000 minus 172,000 minus 13,000 equals 22,000, subject to the example’s assumptions.

### 14. Identify competing interests

Learning goal: Recognize a question for review without deciding priority.

#### The core idea

A former owner’s interest may coexist with interests asserted by lienholders, co-owners, an estate, an assignee, or other parties. The applicable sale and claim process determine how competing interests are resolved. A name on a deed does not settle every question about a particular fund.

#### Put it into practice

Create an issue list from the documents the client is authorized to share. Note the source of each asserted interest, what is confirmed, and what remains contested. Do not rank interests or tell a person they have no rights based on an AI-generated conclusion. Flag inconsistent documents and seek review by the appropriate professional or official process.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

A research assistant can help organize evidence for a decision-maker. It cannot turn an allegation into an order. Distinguish “a claim was asserted” from “a distribution was approved” in both calculations and communications.

Practice: In a fictional file, identify a co-owner question and a disputed lien. Draft a neutral issue list that makes no entitlement decision.

### 15. Choose a responsible next action

Learning goal: Decide whether to proceed administratively, pause, or refer.

#### The core idea

A case can look attractive while missing the facts needed for responsible work. Review five areas before progressing: permitted activity, client authority, reliable evidence, current funds status, and the need for professional representation. An unknown in one area must remain visible even when the other areas look complete.

#### Put it into practice

Use plain next-action labels. “Organize authorized documents” fits a file ready for administrative work. “Await custodian confirmation” fits an unverified balance. “Refer authority or entitlement issue” fits a disputed or complex claim. These labels describe tasks; they are not legal approvals or automatic business scores.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

A small amount is not a reason to skip authority checks, and a large apparent amount is not a reason to ignore restrictions. Record why you paused and what evidence would allow the next permitted step. Protect the client’s interests rather than chasing a numerical target.

Practice: Evaluate the fictional capstone file with one missing authority document and a pending competing claim. Choose two specific next actions.

## Coordinate responsible assistance

### 16. Run a permissioned consultation

Learning goal: Explain facts, unknowns, and optional assistance.

#### The core idea

Begin with identity and permission: who you are, why the person requested the conversation, and what they want help understanding. State that you are not the custodian and cannot promise a disbursement. Keep the explanation grounded in the source documents available for that authorized file.

#### Put it into practice

A neutral example is: “You asked us to help organize your documents. The record we have is dated this month and describes a deposit. We have not yet verified the remaining balance or entitlement. The official office explains the direct process here. Would you like a list of the missing documents for your review?” This is a fictional practice script, not permission to contact strangers.

#### Dr. Gainz checkpoint

Avoid pressure based on fear, a fabricated deadline, or a claim of exclusivity. If a real deadline may exist, identify its source and refer the legal timing question promptly. Ask what the person understood before discussing any locally reviewed service terms.

Practice: Role-play a consultation using fictional facts. Include one confirmed fact, two unknowns, the direct-claim route, and a specific next action.

4. Our AI has approved your entitlement

### 17. Create a document request and handoff packet

Learning goal: Gather only what the next reviewer needs.

#### The core idea

A document request should explain the purpose of each requested item. Use the supplied case reference and ask for copies through a suitable secure channel. #### Put it into practice

A handoff packet can contain an index, client authorization scope, sale/process classification, a dated event log, source-linked accounting figures, unresolved questions, and the next review requested. Flag incomplete or inconsistent material. Never hide a competing claim, an expired-date concern, or an access restriction from the reviewer.

### 18. Coordinate with the appropriate professional

Learning goal: Keep roles and compensation transparent.

#### The core idea

Some situations require legal judgment or representation. Examples include disputed entitlement, estates, entities, competing claims, assignments, and questions about permitted compensation. Requirements vary. A professional handoff should ask a focused question instead of asking someone to validate a conclusion an AI already announced to the client.

#### Put it into practice

### 20. Build a source-constrained case brief

#### The core idea

### 23. Evaluate costs before offering a service

#### The core idea
