## 5. Source-constrained AI case brief
Prompt: Organize only the authorized material I supply. Use a case alias. Create a table with each fact, exact source/document location, date, uncertainty and next verification step. Label unsupported items UNKNOWN. Distinguish sale proceeds, deposit, current balance, entitlement and disbursement. Do not infer priority, draft operative contracts, promise a payout, contact anyone or file anything. End with questions for the responsible office or qualified local professional.
Human review: check every amount, date, quotation, source and inference. Confirm permitted scope before any external action.

## 6. Consultation outline
Ask what assistance the person requested.
Explain the official direct-claim route and voluntary nature of your service.
Describe the specific administrative work you can lawfully provide.
Explain unresolved facts, costs and professional-referral boundaries.
Document the requested next step without pressuring for a signature.

## 7. Professional handoff and pricing questions
Which activities may this business perform in this jurisdiction?
Who may represent the claimant, sign and file each document?
Are fees, assignments, timing or solicitation restricted?
What current official forms and evidence apply?
What must be disclosed, reviewed or independently verified?
Do not use these questions as a substitute for a reviewed agreement.
