# Membership access policy — deployed November 24, 2026

Pro: $50/month, exactly one selected niche, 30% direct membership commissions. Elite: $97/month, all three niches, Business Automation workspace, 50% direct membership commissions. AI, agents, and activated services retain separate metered billing. Elite does not grant unlimited free usage. Existing complimentary exceptions remain administrator-managed and exclude Automation.

Authority is memberEntitlements/{uid} in the private money-masteries-automation database, checked against Firebase Authentication and verified Stripe subscriptions. Default-database membershipAccess/{uid} is a server-written rules mirror. Pro selects once through mmSelectProNiche. The client cannot change billing, payout, referrer or permission fields. No second-level membership commission is created. A payment-key ledger prevents duplicate credits; membership invoice lines alone count, excluding usage, unrelated products, tax and discounts. Existing financial balances and subscriptions were not reset or canceled.
