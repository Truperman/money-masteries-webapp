I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 10: Locate the current custodian.
A source may describe a sale, an initial deposit, or an older holding
account. None automatically identifies where money is held today.
A file can involve a clerk, trustee, treasurer, court registry, or state
agency. Identify the actual custodian and distinguish its accounting role
from the role of a court deciding entitlement.
For an authorized case, request or review the relevant official status
through the applicable process.
Record the response date, source document, balance definition, and whether
any transfer or distribution occurred.
If funds moved, keep both the old and new references.
Do not turn a historical balance into a current one simply because a webpage
still displays it. Use three separate fields: original reported deposit,
current confirmed balance, and verification date.
Leave missing values unknown.
A written current response can still contain conditions or pending matters
that must be preserved in your summary.

What is the current county balance?
The answer: Unknown until current custody and accounting are verified.
A transfer changes the verification task; do not invent either a current
balance or a zero.
Keep the source, the date, and the unknowns visible.
