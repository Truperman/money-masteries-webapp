I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 15: Choose a responsible next action.
A case can look attractive while missing the facts needed for responsible
work. Review five areas before progressing: permitted activity, client
authority, reliable evidence, current funds status, and the need for
professional representation. An unknown in one area must remain visible even
when the other areas look complete.
Use plain next-action labels.
“Organize authorized documents” fits a file ready for administrative work.
“Await custodian confirmation” fits an unverified balance.
“Refer authority or entitlement issue” fits a disputed or complex claim.
These labels describe tasks; they are not legal approvals or automatic
business scores. A small amount is not a reason to skip authority checks,
and a large apparent amount is not a reason to ignore restrictions.
Record why you paused and what evidence would allow the next permitted step.
Protect the client’s interests rather than chasing a numerical target.

Quick knowledge check. Which file is ready to be advertised as a guaranteed
recovery? The answer: None of these establishes a guaranteed recovery.
Administrative readiness, legal entitlement, and eventual payment are
different questions. Keep the source, the date, and the unknowns visible.
