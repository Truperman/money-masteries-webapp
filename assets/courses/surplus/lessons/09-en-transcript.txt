I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 9: Explain the direct-claim option.
People should understand that a government or court process may be available
directly. Explain what your business would add, if the activity and
compensation are permitted. Do not imply you control the custodian, have an
exclusive government relationship, or must be paid before someone can
inquire about their own funds.
A useful consultation statement is: “The official office describes a direct
claim process. I can show you that resource.
If you want help organizing documents or coordinating a permitted review, we
can discuss the scope and any locally reviewed terms.” Tailor facts to the
actual jurisdiction; do not tell a person that every claim is free or every
case needs counsel. Invite questions and allow time to review information.
Distinguish a request for information from a decision to retain assistance.
If there is confusion about fees, authority, or rights, pause instead of
treating urgency as a reason to skip the explanation.
Pause and try this.
Practice a two-minute explanation that includes the official resource,
optional assistance, unresolved facts, and a clear next step.
Quick knowledge check. Which statement should be avoided?
The answer: You must hire us to obtain your own funds.
Do not present optional assistance as a mandatory gateway to a public claim
process. Keep the source, the date, and the unknowns visible.
