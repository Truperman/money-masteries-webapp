I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 11: Read the sale and distribution documents.
A winning bid, an opening bid, a judgment amount, and a deposit balance
describe different things. Start a document index rather than subtracting
whichever two numbers are easiest to find.
Record what each figure represents and its effective date.
Interest, permitted costs, refunds, or later distributions may affect the
accounting. Use a fictional evidence table with columns for document title,
source, date, figure label, amount, and limitation.
Match a sale result to the appropriate official accounting.
A ledger line should be traceable to a source; an AI summary is not the
source itself. When a figure is missing or inconsistent, record a question
instead of filling it with zero.

Quick knowledge check. Which subtraction is sufficient to verify an owner’s
current payout? The answer: Neither without the applicable accounting and
entitlement review. Different figures cannot be treated as interchangeable
or as a verified payable amount.
