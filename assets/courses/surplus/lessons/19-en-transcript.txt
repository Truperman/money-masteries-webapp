I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 19: Use AI to organize authorized
documents. Start an AI task with a permitted purpose and the minimum
authorized material. For example: “Index these fictional documents.
Extract the document date and stated figure label.
Cite the page for each item.
Use unknown for missing information.
Do not infer an owner, contact information, or legal entitlement.” This
turns a broad assistant into a constrained administrative helper.
Review the output against the originals.
AI can confuse a bid with a balance, merge two people, overlook a date
qualifier, or invent a missing field.
Keep an evidence column and a review status.
Do not use a confidence score as a replacement for a source.
Use private storage appropriate to the client material rather than public
website assets or shared marketing files.

Quick knowledge check. What should AI output when an amount is missing?
The answer: Unknown with the missing source identified.
Missing financial information must remain missing until a reliable source
supplies it. Keep the source, the date, and the unknowns visible.
