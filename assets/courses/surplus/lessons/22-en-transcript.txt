Review a fictional case from start to handoff.
Case A is entirely fictional.
An owner initiates contact and authorizes document organization.
A sale result records $225 ,000.
Supplied accounting records show $18 ,000 in costs and a $172
,000 obligation. An older worksheet suggests a $35 ,000
residual. A later document describes a possible $13 ,000 competing
interest, but the supplied material does not establish its disposition or a
current payable balance. Your job is to organize what is known, not decide
that the owner is owed $35 ,000 or $22 ,000.
Prepare the process classification, authorization, authority scope,
document index, event log, illustrative scenarios, and unresolved
questions. Request current custodian information through the appropriate
process and refer the competing interest issue for proper review.
A complete practice submission identifies missing facts openly.
Do not reward yourself for a larger estimated recovery.
Evaluate whether another reviewer could trace the sources and understand
exactly which decisions remain unresolved.
