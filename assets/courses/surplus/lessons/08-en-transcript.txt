I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 8: Verify who has authority to act.
The person who contacts you may be the former owner, a co-owner, a
representative of a business, a relative, or someone assisting an estate.
Those descriptions do not by themselves prove entitlement or authority.
Keep an identity check separate from ownership history and from the right to
receive a particular distribution.
Ask for the specific documents relevant to the next review step through a
secure channel. For an estate, entity, trust, or disputed ownership,
record the issue and seek qualified guidance rather than guessing who can
sign. Do not locate relatives, infer heirs, or decide probate rights through
an AI-generated family tree.
Work from authorized material and appropriate official findings.
Maintain an authority field marked verified for the stated task, awaiting
review, or not established.
Preserve any limitation on sharing.
Stop the workflow when someone seeks action beyond the authority documented
in the file. Pause and try this.
Classify three fictional intake examples: an owner asking about their own
records, an adult child without authority documents, and an entity
