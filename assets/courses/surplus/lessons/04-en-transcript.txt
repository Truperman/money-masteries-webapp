I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 4: Map official programs before
researching cases. Start with official program and procedure pages.
Identify the agency name, sale type, official URL, update date,
contact channel for the office, and any access limitations.
County-level research can help you understand a market without compiling
private individuals into financial prospect profiles.
Keep third-party marketing claims separate from agency evidence.
The companion research directory contains 75 program-source entries across

It is a starting directory, not a list of available claims or cash.
Read each row’s limitations before relying on it.
Separate historic, closed, login-limited, and current-procedure sources.
Recheck dates rather than treating a research snapshot as a continuing feed.
Do not infer “no funds” from an absent web list, or “funds available” from a
link that exists. Record unknown availability honestly.
Public access and permitted commercial use are separate questions.

Quick knowledge check. What does a county program page prove?
The answer: The official source or procedure exists; case facts still
require verification. A program source identifies a process, not current
money or permission to solicit individuals.
Keep the source, the date, and the unknowns visible.
