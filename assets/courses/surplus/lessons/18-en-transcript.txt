I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 18: Coordinate with the appropriate professional.
Some situations require legal judgment or representation.
Examples include disputed entitlement, estates, entities, competing claims,
assignments, and questions about permitted compensation.
Requirements vary. A professional handoff should ask a focused question
instead of asking someone to validate a conclusion an AI already announced
to the client. Explain who performs each task, who communicates with the
custodian, who may sign or file, and how costs are handled under reviewed
terms. Obtain appropriate permission before sharing client information.
Do not promise that a professional will accept a case, that a fee will be
allowed, or that a referral arrangement is lawful without review.
Keep a written responsibility list.
A helpful administrative role can coexist with professional representation,
but boundaries must be explicit.
When a professional requests clarification, update the evidence file and
retain the earlier version rather than silently changing what was supplied.

Quick knowledge check. What should an AI case brief do with a disputed
entitlement? The answer: Flag the sourced dispute for the appropriate
review. A case brief supports review; it does not exercise legal authority.
Keep the source, the date, and the unknowns visible.
