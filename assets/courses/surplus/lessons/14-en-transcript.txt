I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 14: Identify competing interests.
A former owner’s interest may coexist with interests asserted by
lienholders, co-owners, an estate, an assignee, or other parties.
The applicable sale and claim process determine how competing interests are
resolved. A name on a deed does not settle every question about a particular
fund. Create an issue list from the documents the client is authorized to
share. Note the source of each asserted interest, what is confirmed, and
what remains contested. Do not rank interests or tell a person they have no
rights based on an AI-generated conclusion.
Flag inconsistent documents and seek review by the appropriate professional
or official process. A research assistant can help organize evidence for a
decision-maker. It cannot turn an allegation into an order.
Distinguish “a claim was asserted” from “a distribution was approved” in
both calculations and communications.
