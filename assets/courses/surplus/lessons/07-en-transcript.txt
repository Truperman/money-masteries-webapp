I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 7: Start with permissioned intake.
Use an owner-initiated or otherwise permissioned consultation instead of
building a mass list of private people and financial balances.
Explain your business identity and the purpose of the discussion.
Ask what help the person wants before receiving documents.
Consent to a conversation is not consent to market unrelated services,
sign filings, sell information, or access every record connected to that
person. Collect the minimum information needed for the next permitted task.
Record the scope of authorization, the date, permitted sharing, and how it
may be withdrawn. Avoid collecting identity numbers, bank details, or
original documents before a justified need and a suitable secure process
exist. Give the person a way to use the official claim process directly.
A signed intake authorization is an administrative record.
It is not an assignment of fund rights, a power to practice law, or an
entitlement decision. Keep those concepts distinct and have any operative
legal document reviewed locally.
Pause and try this.
Write a short intake-purpose statement and a permission checklist.
Practice with fictional information only.
Quick knowledge check. Does permission to organize supplied documents
authorize signing a court claim?
The answer: No; representation and signing authority require separate
verification. Administrative permission does not establish authority for
legal representation or signatures.
Keep the source, the date, and the unknowns visible.
