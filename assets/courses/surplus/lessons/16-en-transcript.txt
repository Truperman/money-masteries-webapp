I'm Doctor Gainz. Lesson 16: Run a permissioned consultation.
Begin with identity and permission: who you are, why the person requested
the conversation, and what they want help understanding.
State that you are not the custodian and cannot promise a disbursement.
Keep the explanation grounded in the source documents available for that
authorized file. A neutral example is: “You asked us to help organize your
documents. The record we have is dated this month and describes a deposit.
We have not yet verified the remaining balance or entitlement.
The official office explains the direct process here.
Would you like a list of the missing documents for your review?” This is a
fictional practice script, not permission to contact strangers.
Avoid pressure based on fear, a fabricated deadline, or a claim of
exclusivity. If a real deadline may exist, identify its source and refer the
legal timing question promptly.
Ask what the person understood before discussing any locally reviewed
service terms. Pause and try this.
Role-play a consultation using fictional facts.
Include one confirmed fact, two unknowns, the direct-claim route, and a
specific next action. Quick knowledge check.
Which communication is appropriate?
The answer: Here is what the dated source says and what remains unverified.
Communicate verified facts and limits without pressure or false authority.
Keep the source, the date, and the unknowns visible.
